About this game

Your first archipelago is only the beginning. Build a flourishing island empire, master complex production chains, and venture into distant oceans in pursuit of the resources your ambitions demand. Shifting winds, waves and currents will test your ingenuity. Through science and research, learn to read the sea, anticipate its changes, and reach shores that once seemed beyond you. Bring each discovery home, and give your people new possibilities to flourish.

Beyond the first islands

The voyage ahead

Master the sea

Winds and currents change. Storms appear, disrupting your trade network. Smart storage and redundancy planning, a keen eye for emerging patterns, and wise decision making are the tools you start with. Build a network that can weather disruption and keep goods flowing when the sea changes its mind.

Research and explore

The world is full of mysteries. Resources are not the only things you’ll find on these shores, but it will take your brightest minds to make sense of them. Turn discoveries into technologies that improve production, transform navigation, and open possibilities your first settlers could only imagine.

It won’t be easy, but the rewards will pay off in many ways. They’ll help keep your people alive, elevate their standard of living, make the impossible possible, and deepen your understanding of this reality.

Sharing the world

Whether you play alone or with friends, you will find neighbours with ambitions like yours. Build one empire together, or lead neighbouring empires of your own. Limited land and scattered resources give you reasons to cooperate, trade, and compete without war.

You are all wise leaders working toward abundance for all. But whose name will go down as the greatest in our history?
