Privacy note

Your data

This site keeps almost nothing about you. The only personal data it collects is the email address you give the newsletter. Visits are counted anonymously.

The newsletter

Second Sailing is made by Velarion Games. When you sign up, your email address goes to EmailOctopus, the service that stores the list and sends the newsletter for us. It is the same newsletter as on velarion.games: a monthly update on how the game is going.

  • Your address is used only to send you this newsletter. It is never sold or shared.
  • Every email has an unsubscribe link. One click takes you off the list.
  • To have your address removed completely, reply to any newsletter email and ask.

Spam protection

When you start using a sign-up form, the page loads EmailOctopus’s form script. It uses Google reCAPTCHA to check that sign-ups come from people, and it fetches its fonts from Google Fonts. Google receives technical data such as your IP address, and reCAPTCHA may set cookies. None of this loads unless you use a form. Google’s Privacy Policy and Terms of Service apply.

Everything else

To see how many people visit and which pages they read, the site uses Umami, a privacy-friendly analytics service. It sets no cookies and doesn’t identify you or follow you across other sites. It counts page views, time on the site, the site you came from, and your country, browser and device type. Your IP address is not stored (Umami’s privacy policy).

There are no ads and no tracking cookies. Fonts and images come from this site itself. The site is hosted on GitHub Pages, and GitHub logs visitors’ IP addresses to keep the service secure (GitHub’s privacy statement).

Links to other sites, such as the dev log on velarion.games, follow those sites’ own policies.
