Roadmap

Where the game has been, and where it is headed next. The dates ahead are my current targets. They are estimates and can move as the work goes.

Reached Planned course
  1. Reached
  2. Jan 2026

    Kickoff

    Full-time work on the game begins.

  3. Jun 2026

    Foundations in place

    The core systems of the game are built, with a clear design direction for where it is heading.

  4. Aug 2026

    First visual pass done

    After a long stretch of placeholder shapes, the game finally looks like a game.

  5. Oct 2026

    Core gameplay loop complete

    Ships carry goods between island settlements, and the full chain of goals can be played to the end. The first playtests with outside players have begun.

  6. Now
    → Q1 2027
    Under way

    Steam page

    Polishing the core loop and making it look its best, so the first public look is a good one. Then the store page opens and you can add the game to your wishlist.

  7. Planned course
  8. Q2 2027

    Free demo

    A short, free demo on Steam to try the start of the game.

  9. Oct 2027

    Steam Next Fest

    The target is to bring the demo to Steam's festival of upcoming games.

  10. Q1 2028

    Release

    The target for Second Sailing's release on Steam.
